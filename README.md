# DiscPro
Title
DiscPro: A Multi-Vendor Near-Expiry Discount System for Local Small Businesses and Communities

The Problem
Local neighborhood stores and small businesses throw away a massive amount of factory-sealed food (like dairy, bread, and snacks) because it reaches its official expiration date before being sold, causing heavy financial losses. At the same time, budget-conscious residents and low-income families struggle to afford groceries but have no way of knowing when or where local merchants are marking down these items. Existing social media pages are too chaotic, causing available neighborhood discounts to get completely lost or ignored.

The Solution
DiscPro is a website connecting independent community sellers with budget-conscious local buyers. It only allows barcoded, pre-packaged goods with clear factory-stamped expiration dates—completely excluding loose items like fresh vegetables or open restaurant food.

Sellers: Neighborhood mom-and-pop shops, convenience stores, and local bakeries scan a product's barcode and enter the stamped expiry date. The website automatically slashes the price based on time left (e.g., 30% off with a week left, 60% off with 1 day left, etc.).

Buyers: Community members view a live feed of cheap groceries nearby. They tap "Reserve" to lock an item, generating a unique claim QR code with a 45-minute timer. The buyer walks or drives directly to the shop, gets their code scanned, pays over the counter, and picks up their item. Unclaimed items go back on the live feed automatically.

Potential Beneficiaries (The Benefactors)
Small Community Businesses: Independent neighborhood merchants stop throwing away cash, recover lost revenue, and automate their stock clearance.

Local Residents & Families: Households and low-income community members get safe, factory-sealed food at massive discounts, directly lowering their monthly grocery bills.
